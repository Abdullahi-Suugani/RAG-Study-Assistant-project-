# Introduction to Computer Networks

A computer network is a group of interconnected computers and devices that communicate and share resources.

A Local Area Network (LAN) connects devices within a limited geographical area, such as a home, school, or office.

A Wide Area Network (WAN) connects devices or networks across large geographical areas. The internet is a well-known example.

A router forwards data packets between different networks. A switch connects devices within a local network.

The Transmission Control Protocol (TCP) helps provide reliable data delivery. The Internet Protocol (IP) addresses devices and helps route packets to their destinations.

Network protocols define rules that devices follow when communicating.
